Heritage Craft

Traditional Cookware & Earthen Living

Earthen utensils are traditional cookware and serving vessels made from natural clay. They are eco-friendly, non-toxic, and retain the natural flavor of food. Known for their ability to retain heat and provide even cooking, these utensils are widely used in rural and urban kitchens for slow-cooking dishes. Popular types include clay pots, pans, and water jugs. They are biodegradable and promote sustainable living.

Handcrafted Clay Diyas & Festive Light

This handcrafted clay lamp, made from natural terracotta, is a symbol of light, purity, and tradition. Commonly used during festivals like Diwali, it offers a warm, steady glow and brings a touch of serenity to any space. Eco-friendly and biodegradable, it reflects the beauty of traditional craftsmanship and sustainable living.
